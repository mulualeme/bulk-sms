# Changelog

## v1.0

First release.

- **Import contacts** from Excel (`.xlsx`), CSV, TSV or TXT. The phone and name columns are detected automatically, and invalid and duplicate numbers are skipped.
- **Personalised messages:** insert `{First Name}`, `{Name}` or any column from your file, preview each person's message and see its SMS count, with an optional opt-out line.
- **Send from either SIM** with a pause of 3–60 seconds between messages. Sending runs in the background with the screen off.
- **Campaign reports:** each recipient is marked Sent or Failed with the reason. You can pause, resume and retry failed messages.
